Why automation

Ten switches,
or one touch.

A great automation system is not about gadgetry. It is about a wall that looks calm, an evening that arrives on cue, and a house that behaves exactly as it did the day it was commissioned.

Preset moods, one-touch recall

Dining, reading, a party, a quiet night — each mood is composed once, then recalled with a single press, exactly the same every time.

Neat walls, few keypads

One elegant keypad controls a large quantity of lights — no banks of switches, no clutter beside the door of a beautiful room.

Dimming across technologies

Phase-cut, DALI, analogue 0–10 V and relay circuits — different lamp technologies, integrated so they dim together as one composition.

High redundancy

The lighting keeps working even if the controller does not. Resilience is designed in from the first drawing, not added later.

App control, schedules, remote access

Adjust a room from the sofa, set the garden to follow the sun, or check the house from another country.

It stays as it was set up

Settings hold in memory. Years later, the scene you loved is still the scene you get — no drift, no re-programming.

Our story

From the radio era
to the age of light.

Yograj Services has been synonymous with electronics since its inception. Established in the 1940s, we began with radios — the most aspirational products of their era — and have kept a single promise ever since: keep up with the times, never compromise on reliability.

The 1940s

The Radio Era

We opened our doors in the age of the valve radio — then the most aspirational object a home could own. It taught us our first lesson: people don’t buy electronics, they buy what electronics make possible.

The decades after

The Home Electronics Age

Televisions, air conditioners and consumer appliances followed. We served individuals, corporates and trade partners alike — building the relationships and the service discipline that still define us.

The last decade

The Automation Decade

For over ten years we have designed and delivered home automation and digital dimming solutions — optimum systems backed by a responsive after-sales team, because we believe in relationships that outlast renovations.

Today

The Third Generation

Now run by the third generation, every new product is extensively tested in-house before we ever offer it to a client. We learn its reliability and its finest features first — so our answers are faster when you need them.

Behind the wall

Four dimming technologies,
one composition.

Chandeliers, cove LED, garden floods and decorative lamps each want to be dimmed differently. We integrate all of them — so a single scene moves the whole room together, smoothly, with no flicker and no lag.

DIN-H81

Phase-cut dimming

Lights are grouped and dimmed together — now essential for decorative fittings and chandeliers where the lamps behave like bulbs.

8 channel leading edge · 8 × 1.25 A

DIN-DALI-64

DALI

Every driver is individually addressable and reports back — the most precise, most diagnosable way to dim a large architectural installation.

1 × DALI universe · 64 channels · 200 mA bus supply

DIN-F8

Analogue & DALI broadcast

For 0–10 V and PWM drivers — the workhorse behind cove lighting and long runs of architectural LED.

8 channel analogue · 8 × 0–10 V / PWM

DIN-RE8

Relay circuits

Switched loads — exhausts, pumps, garden and utility circuits — brought into the same scenes and the same app.

8 channel relay interface · 8 × 16 A

    Products

    Four ways
    to begin.

    Whether you are building from bare shell or refining a finished home, there is a path that fits — every one of them tested in-house before it is ever offered to you.

    01

    Centralised Solution

    A DIN-rail system: dimmers, relays and fan controllers live discreetly in an automation distribution panel at the back end, giving full flexibility in the number of circuits and easy serviceability. At the front, you touch only beautifully designed keypads.

    Best for — new builds & full renovations

    02

    UNIVO Series

    A glass-panelled module that is both front and back end — no backend panel required. Simplified wiring, easier installation and a long list of features make UNIVO the economical, effective middle path.

    Best for — elegant simplicity, faster installs

    03  NEW

    Go by Raylogic

    The state-of-the-art retrofit. Your existing switches are replaced by GO modules — and with nothing more than Wi-Fi, your space is automated and online. No rewiring, no renovation, no dust sheets.

    Best for — finished homes, zero disruption
